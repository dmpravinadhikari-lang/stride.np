# Stride — Brand quick reference

**One-liner:** The AI-powered operating system for education consultancies.
**Tagline:** Run your consultancy on intelligence, not instinct.

**Description (use everywhere):** Stride is the AI-powered operating system for education consultancies. It brings every part of the business — leads, admissions, classes and attendance, IELTS/PTE mock tests, AI mock interviews, SOPs and CVs, scholarships, HR and payroll — into one system, and turns that data into decisions: which lead to call next, which student is falling behind, which branch needs help. Built for consultancies that want to run like the best in the world.

## Logo
Three rotated squares (footsteps) on a 45° rising diagonal, sized 1 : φ : φ². Pink = enquiry, Orange = preparation, Yellow = departure.
- Files: `stride-mark.svg` (full colour), `stride-mark-white.svg` (on orange/pink/yellow/photo), `stride-mark-navy.svg` (mono print).
- Lockup: mark left, wordmark "Stride" in Outfit 600, tracking −3.5%. Mark height : cap height = φ. Gap = mark ÷ φ².
- Clear space = mark ÷ φ. Min lockup 100px; min mark 16px.
- Never: stretch, recolour, rotate, or full-colour on orange/pink/yellow grounds.

> Corrected in build: #B85C00 measures 4.41:1 on Paper and 3.97:1 on Mist, which
> fails the standard it was specified to meet. The product uses #A85300, the same
> hue two steps darker, and white sits on it at 5.38:1 for button fills.

Rules: Paper + Ink do the work; one orange element per view; white text on orange only ≥24px; never Ink on Orange or White on Yellow; only permitted gradient is pink→orange→yellow as a thin rule or ≤15% glow.

## Type
Outfit (400/500/600/700) for everything; JetBrains Mono 400 for IDs, scores, money, timestamps. Display 32/600 · Heading 20/600 · Body 15/400 · Label 12/500 uppercase. Sentence case everywhere. No exclamation marks.

## Voice
Plain, specific, human, facts not slogans. "Students, counsellors, branches" not "users". AI copy says what it decided ("Writing is the one holding your average down"), never "AI-powered" on a button.

## Modules → colour group
- Grow (pink): Student leads, Students board, Automatic emails, Scholarship finder, Market, Universities & partners, Parent view
- Prepare (orange): Attendance, AI class prep, IELTS/PTE mocks, AI mock interview, SOP Studio, CV maker, Document vault, Checklist, True cost calculator
- Run (yellow): Staff & teams, Payroll, Auto employee assessment, Employee tracking, Offices, Reports
